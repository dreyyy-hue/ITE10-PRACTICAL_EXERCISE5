Garlic Butter Pasta

A quick and comforting pasta tossed with fragrant garlic and butter.

Preparation: 10 minutesCooking: 15 minutesServes: 2

Ingredients

  • 200 g spaghetti
  • 3 tablespoons butter
  • 4 cloves garlic, minced
  • 2 tablespoons grated cheese
  • 2 tablespoons chopped parsley
  • Salt and black pepper to taste
  • 1/4 cup pasta water

Instructions

  1. Cook the spaghetti in salted boiling water according to the package directions.
  2. Before draining, reserve about 1/4 cup of pasta water.
  3. Melt butter in a pan over low heat.
  4. Add garlic and cook briefly until fragrant. Do not let it burn.
  5. Add the cooked pasta and a little pasta water. Toss until coated.
  6. Season with salt and pepper, then finish with cheese and parsley.
