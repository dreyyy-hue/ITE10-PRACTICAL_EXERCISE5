Chicken Adobo

A classic Filipino dish with tender chicken simmered in a savory, tangy sauce.

Preparation: 15 minutesCooking: 40 minutesServes: 4

Ingredients

  • 1 kg chicken pieces
  • 1/2 cup soy sauce
  • 1/2 cup vinegar
  • 1 cup water
  • 6 cloves garlic, crushed
  • 2 bay leaves
  • 1 teaspoon whole peppercorns
  • 1 tablespoon cooking oil

Instructions

  1. Combine chicken, soy sauce, garlic, and peppercorns in a bowl. Marinate for at least 20 minutes.
  2. Heat oil in a pot and lightly brown the chicken.
  3. Pour in the marinade, vinegar, water, and bay leaves.
  4. Bring to a boil. Avoid stirring for the first few minutes after adding vinegar.
  5. Lower the heat, cover, and simmer for about 30 minutes or until the chicken is cooked through.
  6. Adjust the sauce to taste and serve hot with rice.
